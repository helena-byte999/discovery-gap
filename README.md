# Discovery Gap

**Do lesser-known artists get a fair chance on Spotify?** They get a fair first listen. They just don't get a second one.

An interactive product case study by Ena: 1.68M plays from 10 friends' Spotify exports, analysed in MySQL, plus four listener interviews. The site walks through the findings and proposes "Second Listen", a way to bring back a lesser-known artist the listener liked once.

**Live site:** https://helena-byte999.github.io/discovery-gap/

The site is a single `index.html` with no build step. Listeners appear only as random codes (L01–L10), and no raw listening data is included.

Independent, not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify. Artist data from Last.fm and Deezer.
