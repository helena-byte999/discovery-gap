B1//ProposalNo new screens

Second Listen.

When a liked lesser-known artist hasn't come back after two weeks, bring them back once, with a different song, in the radio, autoplay or mix where the listener first heard them.

PrimaryA different song by the triggered artist, played and not skipped, in days 30–90. The re-introduced song doesn't count.

Decision ruleShip if the primary metric rises and no guardrail breaks. Iterate on timing or song choice if only the leading metric moves. Stop if re-introductions are skipped more than the surface's normal rate.

B4//ReflectionLimits

What I learned

  • The most useful moment was the hypothesis failing. Following the data past the first listen gave a sharper, more buildable problem.
  • A pooled number nearly misled me. My first draft headlined "skipped less: 7.9% vs 17.8%". A peer review showed that mostly reflects who listens. Now every comparison is within listeners first.
  • Measurement took most of the time. Ranking within genre, checked by hand against Spotify, stopped Burna Boy from counting as "mid".
  • Interviews need neutral questions. Where I raised "popularity bias" myself, the answer isn't used as evidence.

Limits

  • 10 friends, mostly heavy listeners in Nigeria and the UK. One has 27% of the plays.
  • "Lean-back" mixes Spotify's surfaces with the listener's own playlists. "Liked" means not skipped.
  • A "first listen" is the first in the export. Big artists may have been heard before.
  • Observational data can't see recommendations shown but not played.
//In one line

Fair first listen.

Build the second.

Spotify already sells artists a way to reach listeners who met them passively. Second Listen does it organically, for every lesser-known artist, in the two weeks that matter.
